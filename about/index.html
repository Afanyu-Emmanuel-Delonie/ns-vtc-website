Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To become a leading vocational and safety training center recognized for developing skilled, competent and job-ready professionals who contribute positively to society and economic development.

Our Mission

To provide quality, practical and industry-relevant vocational and safety training that equips learners with the technical knowledge, practical skills and confidence needed for employment, entrepreneurship and professional development.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide how we train, support and prepare every learner at NS-VTC.

Quality

Maintaining high standards in training and skills development.

Practical Excellence

Promoting learning through real activities and practical experience.

Safety

Encouraging safe working practices and responsibility.

Integrity

Promoting honesty, discipline and professionalism.

Inclusiveness

Providing equal learning opportunities for all.

Innovation

Encouraging creativity, problem-solving and continuous improvement.

Commitment

Supporting learners in achieving their educational and career goals.
