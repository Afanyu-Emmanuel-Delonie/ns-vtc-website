What We Cover

Training Areas

We recognise that a skilled workforce is essential for productivity, efficiency and safety. Our training is tailored to your organisation’s needs.

Occupational Health and Safety

Support employees in understanding workplace hazards, risk prevention, PPE and safe working procedures.

Construction Safety

Develop awareness of construction site hazards, working at heights, scaffolding safety and general site safety practices.

Fire Safety and Emergency Preparedness

Improve employee awareness of fire prevention, evacuation procedures and emergency response.

Workplace Risk Assessment

Introduce employees to hazard identification, risk assessment and appropriate preventive measures.

Technical Skills Development

Support workforce development through practical training in scaffolding, steel fixing, welding, masonry, carpentry and painting.

Employee Skills Improvement

Provide opportunities for employees to improve their technical knowledge, practical competence and professional confidence.

Entry Requirements

NS-VTC is open to anyone ready to learn. Here is what to expect before you join.

Age

Open to young people and adults seeking skills development.

Education

No specific academic qualifications required for most TVET programs.

Language

Training is conducted in Kinyarwanda and English.

Commitment

Full attendance and participation in practical sessions is expected.

PPE

Trainees must wear appropriate personal protective equipment during practical sessions.

Accommodation

Stay On Campus

Accommodation is available for both male and female trainees. Contact us to confirm availability and any associated costs before your intake date.
